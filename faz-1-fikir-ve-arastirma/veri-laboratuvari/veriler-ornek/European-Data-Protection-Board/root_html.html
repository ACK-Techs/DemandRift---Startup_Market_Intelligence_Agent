Right to privacy in a digital world

In our digital age, where information is constantly shared, collected and processed, clear and strong data protection rules are needed. They ensure that individuals stay in control of their data, and safeguard their personal freedom, autonomy and freedom of expression. Data protection rules are also crucial to prevent misuse, unauthorised access, identity theft and manipulation. Strong data protection safeguards build trust in online services.
