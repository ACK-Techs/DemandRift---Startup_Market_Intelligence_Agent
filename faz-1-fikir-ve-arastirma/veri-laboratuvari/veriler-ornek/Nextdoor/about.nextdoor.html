      If it's happening in your neighborhood, it's on Nextdoor.

      About Nextdoor

      Nextdoor is the essential neighborhood network for 110 million verified neighbors across 350,000 neighborhoods, offering trusted local news, real-time safety alerts, neighbor recommendations, for sale and free listings, and local events. Nextdoor connects neighbors to the people, places, and information that matter most in their local communities. In addition, local businesses, news publishers, and public agencies use Nextdoor to share important information and engage with neighbors at scale.

      Our Mission and Purpose

      Our mission is to activate the power of local. Our purpose is to make every neighborhood feel like home.

      We do this by connecting neighbors to the local gems around them – people, places, and information. These local connections improve our lives by fostering a sense of community and belonging no matter where we live.

      Your Essential Neighborhood Network

      Nextdoor is the place for what’s happening in your neighborhood. Trusted, timely, useful information that helps you stay in the know and share what you know about your neighborhood. Here’s how:

      News: Stay Informed with Trusted Local Sources

      Know the stories shaping your neighborhood. From a new proposed bike lane to the high school team's recent championship win, News brings you relevant stories from vetted local publishers directly into your feed. With over 4,000 local publications across the US, UK, and Canada, we make it easier than ever to join the conversations happening in your community.

      Alerts: Stay Safe and Prepared

      Receive real-time alerts to keep you and your family safe. From daily traffic updates to urgent notifications about severe storms or power outages, Alerts keep you connected during critical moments. Powered by trusted sources and in partnership with local public agencies like fire departments, we deliver the timely information you need.   

      Ask: Discover Local Favorites

      Uncover the hidden gems your neighbors love. Ask uses AI technology to distill insights from years of neighborhood conversations to help you find the best of your community. Whether you’re looking for “the best place to hike with kids” or a trusted local plumber, Ask gives you quick, summarized recommendations from the people who know best–your neighbors.

      For Sale & Free: Find Neighborhood Treasures

      Declutter your home or find something new from right around the corner. Give a second-hand table a new life, sell your old bike to a neighbor down the street, or find free items for your home. Nextdoor’s marketplace is built on trust and powered by community. 

      Events and Groups: Where Neighbors Get Together

      Turn shared interests into real-life connections. From a weekly pickup game to a neighborhood-wide garage sale, you can use Nextdoor to organize events and connect with neighbors who share your interests.  

      Discover Community

      We all want to know what’s happening around us…and be a part of it. Which is why we built Nextdoor. 

      It’s where you can get a recommendation for a favorite handyman, find out what’s next for that vacant corner lot, or get a timely alert when a storm is drawing near. Nextdoor is the place to stay in the know about what’s happening around you, and a place to share what you know, too.

      We know that when neighbors start talking, communities get stronger. These connections, from everyday updates to unexpected moments, are what help turn a location into a place you love to be. This is how we help ensure that for our neighbors, here… becomes home.
