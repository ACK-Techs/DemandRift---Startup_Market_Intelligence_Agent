Finding fit

Matching trained on ten years of real studies

Each study makes four passes, trading off fit, fill speed, and the long-term health of the panel.

Hard requirements first

Requirements like age, location, and role quietly remove anyone who can’t qualify before they ever reach your screener.

Ranked by likelihood to qualify

Trained on millions of past screeners, the model ranks each person by how genuinely likely they are to pass yours.

Proven participants prioritized

Reliable, well-rated members rise to the top - especially on sensitive or hard-to-fill studies.

Live tracking of fill rate and quality

Detecting fraud

Fraud keeps changing.

Four independent checkpoints catch bad actors at different depths - long before anything reaches your results.

At sign-up

New members are weighed against 50+ signals on day one, from identity and device fingerprints to work-email verification.

Around each study

Briefs with broad criteria or quick screeners are flagged as higher-risk and steered toward established participants.

At your review

You see ratings and activity history, video screening, verification badges, and flags for copy-paste or AI-written answers.

With a real coordinator

On managed recruits, a person investigates anything suspicious, swaps out bad fits, and refunds you - no chasing required.
